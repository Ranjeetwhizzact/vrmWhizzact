# Company User (Staff) - Step-by-Step Manual

This guide provides a walkthrough for Staff/Company Users responsible for patient coordination and data management.

---

## Step 1: Searching for Patient Records
1. Log in to your account.
2. Go to the **Customers** or **Patients** section.
3. Use the **Search Bar** to find patients by:
    *   Proposal Number
    *   Mobile Number
    *   Full Name
4. You can also use **Filters** to see patients from a specific branch or date range.

## Step 2: Tracking Communication (Adding Logs)
It is important to keep a record of every interaction with a patient.
1. Click the **View** icon next to a patient's name.
2. Scroll to the **Logs** section.
3. Select the **Status** (e.g., "In Progress", "Not Answering", "Confirmed").
4. Type a **Comment** summarizing your conversation (e.g., "Customer asked to call back at 5 PM").
5. Click **Add Log**. This information will be visible to the Admin and Doctor.

## Step 3: Handling Bulk Data
If you receive a list of patients from an insurance company:
1. Go to **Bulk Upload**.
2. Select the Excel/CSV file provided to you.
3. Click **Upload**.
4. If there are errors, download the error report, fix the data in your Excel file, and re-upload the corrected rows.

## Step 4: Exporting Information
If an insurance company or branch manager needs a report:
1. Apply the necessary filters (e.g., "Today's Completed Cases").
2. Click the **Export to Excel** or **Export to Word** button at the top of the list.
3. The file will download immediately to your computer.

## Step 5: Coordinating Availability
If a patient has not been scheduled yet:
1. Send the **Availability Form** link to the patient via the system.
2. Once the patient submits their preferred date/time, it will appear in their profile.
3. Notify the Admin to finalize the schedule with a doctor.
