# Doctor - Step-by-Step Manual

This guide provides a walkthrough for Medical Examiners (Doctors) using the Honest Health Care System.

---

## Step 1: Login & Initial Setup
1. Log in to the portal using your registered **Email** and **Password**.

## Step 2: Checking Your Schedule
1. On your Dashboard, you will see a list of **Assigned Patients**.
2. Review the appointment date and time for each patient.
3. You can click on a patient's name to see their basic information and any documents they have uploaded (ID proof, etc.).

## Step 3: Joining the Video Examination
1. At the scheduled time, find the patient in your list.
2. Click the **Join Meeting** button.
3. The Zoom interface will open.
    *   Ensure your camera and microphone are turned on.
    *   Greet the patient and verify their identity.
4. Conduct the medical examination as per the required protocol.

## Step 4: Writing the Medical Report
1. Once the call is finished, click on **Create Report** next to the patient's name.
2. Fill out the medical findings in the digital form.
3. **Prescriptions:** If required, add medicine details in the prescription section.
4. Click **Submit Report**.
5. The system will automatically generate a PDF report with your signature and findings.

## Step 5: Reviewing History
1. Navigate to the **Report History** tab.
2. Here you can find all your past consultations and download the generated PDF reports for your own records.
